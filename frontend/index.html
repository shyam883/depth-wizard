1. Perspective Height Mathematics

Height of an object \(H_{est}\) is derived from its base depth \(Z_{base}\), pixel height \(\Delta y\), and camera focal length \(f_y\):

\(H_{est} = \frac{Z_{base} \cdot \Delta y}{f_y \cdot \cos(\theta_{pitch})}\)

Where \(\theta_{pitch}\) is the camera tilt angle inferred from vanishing points.

2. Scale Ambiguity Resolution

Monocular depth is inherently up-to-scale. Scale is calibrated using standard reference semantic priors:

  • Standard vehicle height reference: ~1.5 m
  • Standard residential doorway: ~2.1 m
  • Standard building story height: ~3.2 m
  • Two-lane road width: ~7.0 m
